StudentPulse collects student feedback through Check-ins. You can organize collection with Groups, add context with Attributes, and manage individual records with Participants. Data exports send feedback to your reporting tools.

## Understand the records

| Concept | Purpose | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Group](/integrations/groups) | A real unit with a permanent Check-in link. | Biology 1A. |
| [Attribute](/integrations/attributes) | A category and value used as context. | Campus: North. |
| [Participant](/integrations/participants) | An individual contact record with memberships. | A student assigned to Biology 1A. |
| Reply | The answers submitted in one Check-in session. | A student's completed feedback. |

A Group can have many Participants and Attributes. A Participant can belong to several Groups. Reply context records the values collected with the feedback.
